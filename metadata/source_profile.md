# Source Dataset Profile

- **Dataset Name**: Stroke Prediction Dataset
- **Primary Domain**: Clinical Cardiovascular & Cerebrovascular Risk
- **Total Ingested Records**: 5,110 patients
- **Total Features**: 12 (demographic, lifestyle, clinical laboratory proxies)
- **Target Label**: `stroke` (binary: 0 = No Stroke, 1 = Stroke)
- **Class Imbalance Ratio**: ~20:1 (Healthy to Stroke cases)
- **Target Cohort Focus**: Adults aged 35 to 90 years old
